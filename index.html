01 / Profile

About

A research-led engineering practice grounded in structural behaviour, material performance and practical context.

I am a structural engineer from Nepal with postgraduate training in Structural Engineering. My academic and research work has focused on the seismic response of reinforced-concrete buildings, computational assessment, finite-element modelling and fibre-reinforced polymer strengthening.

My M.Tech research involved a 54-case parametric study comparing the seismic response of reinforced-concrete buildings under IS 1893:2016 and IS 1893:2025, using ETABS and Python-based regression analysis. I have also worked on ABAQUS modelling of structural components and experimental investigations of GFRP-confined concrete.

At Pro Eth Pvt. Ltd., I contribute to research on unstabilised, stabilised and reinforced rammed-earth systems, including material characterisation and experimental planning for sustainable construction in Nepal.

02 / Selected work

Research & projects

Selected research themes and investigations. Project descriptions are intentionally limited to work that can be publicly summarised.

01 — CURRENT RESEARCH

Stabilised and Reinforced Rammed Earth

Research at Pro Eth on unstabilised, stabilised and reinforced rammed-earth systems, including soil and material characterisation, stabilisation strategies and experimental planning for Nepalese construction contexts.

Sustainable construction · Material characterisation
02 — M.TECH RESEARCH

Comparative Seismic Assessment of RC Buildings

A 54-case parametric study using ETABS to compare building response under IS 1893:2016 and IS 1893:2025, with Python-based dataset preparation and regression analysis.

Earthquake engineering · ETABS · Python
03 — COMPUTATIONAL STUDY

Seismic Assessment and CFRP Retrofitting

Computational assessment of a vertically expanded reinforced-concrete building using ETABS and ABAQUS, including nonlinear modelling of a representative beam–column assembly and evaluation of CFRP strengthening.

Structural assessment · ABAQUS · CFRP
04 — EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH

GFRP-Confined Concrete

Experimental investigation of GFRP-confined concrete specimens, examining compressive and split-tensile behaviour, deformation response and observed failure characteristics.

Experimental mechanics · GFRP
05 — ONGOING PROJECT

Image-Based Concrete Crack Assessment

Exploration of a CNN-based approach for detecting and classifying visible cracks in concrete structures, with image-based damage characterisation as a potential input to condition assessment.

Computer vision · Machine learning
